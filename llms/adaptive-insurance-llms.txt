# Adaptive Insurance

> Adaptive Insurance is a specialty MGA and insurtech platform offering parametric and specialty insurance for climate and weather risk in the United States. Products cover power outages, wind and hail deductible exposure, and residential flood. Policies are quoted and bound through agents and brokers, typically in under five minutes.

## Products

- [GridProtect Power Outage Coverage](https://www.adaptiveinsurance.com/product/gridprotect-power-outage-coverage): Parametric power outage insurance that pays automatically when the grid goes down, typically within days.
- [Wind-Hail Deductible Buy-Back - Commercial](https://www.adaptiveinsurance.com/product/wind-hail-deductible-buy-back-commercial): Reduces business out-of-pocket wind and hail deductible exposure, buy down to as low as $5K. Available in all 50 states.
- [Wind-Hail Deductible Buy-Back - Residential](https://www.adaptiveinsurance.com/product/wind-hail-deductible-buy-back-residential): Reduces homeowner out-of-pocket wind and hail deductible exposure, buy down to as low as $2,500. Available in all 50 states.
